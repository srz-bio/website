srz bio is a three-person biotech company in Toronto, Vancouver and San Francisco.

Many serious diseases have a well-understood cause and still have no treatment, often because the patient group is too small for large companies to prioritize.

We think a small team can close that gap. We run our own experiments, pick programs where the biology points clearly to a mechanism, and partner early so treatments reach patients sooner.

We are three people. We intend to stay that way.
